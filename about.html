About LTC Sentinel

LTC Sentinel is an independent data journalism platform focused exclusively on the long-term care industry. We analyze publicly available CMS datasets to surface trends, identify at-risk facilities, and track the ownership changes reshaping American nursing homes.

Our mission is simple: make the data accessible. CMS publishes tens of thousands of data points across dozens of datasets. We connect the dots so that industry professionals, policymakers, advocates, and families can understand what's really happening.
